How to read this report

  • Decision rule. Every column gets one p-value from its decision test; these, the missing-rate tests and the domain classifier form one family, corrected with bh at alpha = 0.05.
  • Drift is not damage. A drifted input the model barely uses can be harmless, and a change in the input-output relationship (concept shift) is invisible to input tests. Check prediction drift and, once labels arrive, the performance section.
  • On alarm: look at the top rows and the domain-classifier importances, rule out a data-pipeline fault (constant values, unit changes, missing spikes) before retraining, and estimate the performance impact before acting.
